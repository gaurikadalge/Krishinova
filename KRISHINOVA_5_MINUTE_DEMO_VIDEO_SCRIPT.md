### Spoken Voiceover Script:

>

> *KrishiNova’s objective is to deliver an in-silico, zero-hardware Digital Twin that conserves 34,000 litres of water per plot, retains over 90% crop yield under drought shocks, and eliminates digital literacy barriers with voice chat in 22 regional languages."*

  - *Screen view:* Show the **Adjust Scenario Variables** sliders on the left (Rainfall Anomaly, Temperature Offset, Usable Tank Storage, Working Budget) and the **Root-Zone Soil Moisture & Crop Water Deficit Curve** on the right.

  - *Action 2:* Hover the mouse along the Recharts curve:

    - Trace the red line (*Water Deficit Stress*) climbing.

  - *Action 1:* Point out **Strategy A (Status Quo - Flood Irrigation)**: Highlight the red badge **`INFEASIBLE`** because it requires 52,000 Litres, which breaches the farmer's 48,000-litre tank storage!
  - *Action 2:* Point out **Strategy B (Deficit Drip + Organic Sugarcane Bagasse Mulching)**: Highlight the glowing green card **`Recommended Optimal`**. ### Spoken Voiceover Script:
> *"Now, let us examine KrishiNova’s primary technical breakthrough: the **Digital Twin & What-If Scenario Lab**.*

>

## Technical Recording & Production Checklist

| Category | Recommended Setting | Pro-Tip for Higher Score |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Screen Resolution** | 1920 × 1080 (1080p, 60fps) | Use OBS Studio or Windows Game Bar (`Win + G`). |
| **Browser Zoom** | 100% or 110% on `https://krishinova-sand.vercel.app` | Ensure top headers and decision engine cards are cleanly visible. |
| **Microphone Audio** | Clear headset/condenser mic with noise suppression | Avoid background fan/room echo; keep voice enthusiastic and clear. |
| **Audio Playback** | System Audio enabled in screen recorder | Test that the Marathi/Hindi voice response from the website is clearly captured in the recording! |
| **Mouse Cursor** | Large yellow highlight or smooth mouse movement | Avoid erratic cursor shaking; deliberately pause on key figures (25mm line, 34,000 L, ₹8,500). |
